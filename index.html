Club History

Real Madrid Club de Futbol, more commonly known as Real Madrid, is the most successful football club in the world, Spain, and Europe. Founded in 1902, the club has won a record 36 La Liga titles, 19 Copa del Rey trophies, and 15 UEFA Champions League titles, and phenomenal players such as Cristiano Ronaldo, Karim Benzema, Luka Modric, and Sergio Ramos. The club's iconic white kit and Santiago Bernabeu Stadium are a testament to its legacy in the world of football.

On March 6, 1902, the club was founded as "Madrid Football Club," and King Alfonso XIII granted its royal title (real meaning royal in Spanish) changing the name to what we know today: Real Madrid. In the 1931-32 season, the club won its first La Liga, starting the beginning of an era of dominance, prestige, and glory. After the Spanish Civil War in 1943, the new club president Santiago Bernabeu Yeste built the iconic Santiago Bernabeu Stadium, bearing his name in his honour.

Following into the modern era, Real Madrid continued its legacy of dominance and persistence. During the Galacticos era, world renowned stars such as Zinedine Zidane (future manager), David Beckham, Ronaldo Nazario (R9), and Luis Figo were signed. Finally, in 2009, the man the myth the legend, Crisitano Ronaldo was signed, making his debut. Ronaldo carried Real Madrid, he was so good, in fact, Zidane implemented a strategy known as "pass to Ronaldo." Thanks to him, Ronaldo led Madrid to three consecutive UCLs.

Since 2020, Real Madrid has continued to be a dominant force in football, from its 15th UCL in 2024, to signing new stars such as Kylian Mbappe, Vinicius Junior, Arda Guler, Jude Bellingham, Valverde, Rodrygo, Yan Diomande, and many more. Recently, on 26 October, Real Madrid beat its historical rival FC Barcelona in a 2-1 victory, showing its continued competitiveness and class in "El Clasico."
